Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reschedule or cancel a session?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 12 hours before your session from the "My Sessions" tab. Late cancellations incur a $25 fee.

What should I wear for a cryo session?

Bring warm socks, gloves, and a towel. Remove all metal jewelry before entering the chamber. We provide protective footwear and gloves.

Is cryotherapy safe for everyone?

Cryo is safe for most people, but not recommended for those with certain heart conditions, severe hypertension, or pregnancy. Please consult with our team for a personal assessment.

How many sessions should I do per week?

Most members see best results with 2–3 sessions per week. Your specialist will recommend a personalized schedule based on your goals.
