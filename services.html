Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Still have questions? Our team is always happy to help — just reach out before your first visit.

Is cryotherapy safe?

Yes — whole-body cryotherapy is considered safe for most healthy adults. Sessions are short (2–3 minutes), and a certified specialist monitors you throughout. We screen for contraindications during your first visit.

How many sessions do I need?

Many clients feel benefits after one session. For chronic pain or athletic recovery, we typically recommend 2–3 sessions per week for several weeks. Your specialist will build a personalized plan.

What should I wear?

For whole-body cryo, we provide protective gloves, socks, and slippers. You'll wear dry, comfortable undergarments or athletic wear. Our team will guide you through preparation before your session.

Can I combine services in one visit?

Absolutely. Many clients pair cryotherapy with compression therapy or infrared sauna for enhanced results. Our staff can recommend the best combination for your goals.
